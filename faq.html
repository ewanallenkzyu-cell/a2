Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers regarding oversized parka fit, Ventile weatherproofing, thermal liner compatibility, cleaning procedures, and salon fittings.

The authentic M-51 fishtail parka was engineered as a combat over-garment designed to fit comfortably over thick wool combat jackets, equipment webbing, and button-in alpaca wool liners without restricting shoulder mobility. When choosing your size, ordering your standard chest size will provide this authentic, flowing military silhouette that drapes handsomely over tailored suits or heavy knitwear. If you prefer a trimmer, contemporary streetwear profile, we recommend sizing down one full size.
Ventile was invented in Manchester during World War II for Royal Air Force fighter pilots ditching in freezing Atlantic waters. It is woven from extra-long-staple cotton fibers spun so densely that there are virtually no microscopic gaps between threads. When exposed to liquid water, the natural cotton cellulose fibers absorb a tiny amount of moisture and swell laterally by up to 20%, completely closing the weave pores and preventing water penetration while remaining exceptionally breathable.
The split rear fishtail hem features two long cotton drawcords and heavy brass snap fasteners. In extreme freezing winds or driving rain, the wearer can fold the split tails forward between their legs and tie them securely around each upper thigh. This prevents the hem from billowing open and creates a sealed thermal chamber that traps body heat while preventing sub-zero wind chills from entering the lower torso. When not in use, the fishtail snaps up cleanly into the interior hem.
Waxed cotton jackets must never be machine-washed, dry-cleaned, or exposed to detergents, as chemical soap dissolves the protective wax matrix. To clean a waxed coat, simply sponge down the exterior with cold clean water and a stiff bristle brush, then allow it to air dry naturally. For pure Ventile parkas, gentle hand washing with cold water and pure soap flakes is permissible, though spot-cleaning is always recommended to preserve the natural fiber tension.
