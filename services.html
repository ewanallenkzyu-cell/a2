Atelier Outerwear Care

Lifetime Garment Servicing & Restoration

Our commitment to your jacket endures across decades of adventures. Discover our specialized outerwear spa services.

Gentle surface cleansing and hand-applied hot beeswax re-proofing to restore full water repellency and rich patina across waxed jackets.
