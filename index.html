The Architecture of Protection

Why the Fishtail Hem Remains Unrivaled

The iconic split rear hem—christened the 'fishtail'—was engineered by US Army Quartermaster designers during the harsh winter campaigns of 1950–1951. When unbuttoned and tied around the upper thighs with integrated cotton drawcords, the fishtail creates an impenetrable windproof seal that prevents freezing ground updrafts from chilling the soldier's core.

In the 1960s, British Mod subculture adopted the surplus parka as an essential sartorial shield, wearing it over tailored mohair suits while riding Lambretta and Vespa scooters through rainy London streets. Today, our atelier refines this functional silhouette with architectural tailoring, weatherproof natural fibers, and removable thermal liners.
