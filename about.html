Outerwear Archive & Heritage

The Art of Mil-Spec Outerwear

Established at 181 Mercer Street, Fishtail Outer was created to rescue authentic military utility garments from ephemeral fashion trends, constructing outerwear engineered to outlive its wearer across decades of harsh meteorological trials.

Manufacturing Philosophy

Form Follows Severe Cold Weather Function

In the sub-zero winters of the Chosin Reservoir during the Korean War, standard issue field uniforms failed catastrophically. The United States Army Quartermaster Corps responded with the EX-48 and subsequently the finalized M-1951 Cold Weather Ensemble: a voluminous, windproof cotton shell designed to be layered over wool combat uniforms and thermal alpaca liners.

Every design element of the parka served a survival function: the heavy copper-plated wire sewn into the hood perimeter allowed soldiers to mold a rigid tunnel against freezing blizzards; the fleece-lined pocket bags thawed frozen fingers; and the split fishtail hem tied around each leg to prevent heat dissipation during movement.

At our Mercer Street atelier, our tailors dissect archival military garments down to their stitch counts, reproducing historical flat-felled seam architectures while upgrading shells to 100% organic weatherproof Ventile and British waxed cotton.

The Pillars of Outerwear Integrity

Our Uncompromising Construction Benchmarks

01

Zero Membrane Delamination

We reject synthetic PTFE membranes that crack, delaminate, and end up in landfills after three seasons. Our Ventile cotton relies on natural fiber swelling to achieve permanent waterproofness.

02

Solid Brass Talon Hardware

Every zipper, snap fastener, and grommet is cast in solid brass and coated in matte gunmetal or blackened oxide to prevent corrosion in marine and alpine salt air.

03

Lifetime Re-Waxing & Repair

Every jacket leaving our atelier comes with lifetime repair privileges. Bring your jacket to our Manhattan flagship for seam re-stitching, patch reinforcement, or seasonal wax re-proofing.
